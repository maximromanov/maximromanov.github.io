Teaching

Courses in history and digital methods taught in Hamburg, Vienna, Leipzig, Boston, and Ann Arbor; intensive workshops and summer schools; and the theses I have supervised.

My teaching combines substantive historical inquiry with methodological literacy. I see training in computational approaches not as a technical specialization but as an extension of core historical skills: working critically with sources, evaluating evidence, and formulating historically grounded arguments. Methodological training is embedded in real research questions rather than taught in isolation, and students work with real historical materials, moving between interpretive reading and structured analysis. Most of my methods courses are explicitly transferable across periods and regional specializations, and several of them are published as open course websites linked below.
